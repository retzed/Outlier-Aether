## Page 23

Additional information

For Everyone

➔Do not task on Multimango without tracking your time!

➔You can task every day from Monday until Sunday, starting at

5 a.m. PT.

➔Payments happen in the night from Tuesday to Wednesday

PT.

➔All subprojects are paid with the same hourly rate.

➔The amount of subprojects available depends on the client.

➔You can task on any available project on Multimango.

Tracking your time correctly and delivering great quality are
very important to stay on the project and avoid payment issues.
